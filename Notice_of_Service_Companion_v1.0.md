### Provenance notes

The baseline is the instrument bearing the stated date above, as reproduced in the current Drive copy. This review does not certify that today’s pre-addendum text is identical to a historical filing snapshot.

An undated paragraph beginning “The record supports a narrow, functional finding” appears after the instrument’s sign-off and before Addendum I. Keep it separately identifiable as an undated explanatory paragraph; its placement alone does not establish its author, insertion date or inclusion in the original filing. It discusses the deletion inference and the absence of an underlying exchange or revision history in the notice. This companion neither removes it nor promotes it into a new amendment.

Neither addendum states its own filing date. The Drive modification timestamp is a timestamp for the current file, not a separate filing date for either amendment.

Addendum I refers to a previously identified triad, but the baseline text inspected does not enumerate that Dedun / Amun / Renenutet triad. Preserve the addendum’s correction without reconstructing missing earlier wording or treating the baseline’s “Name: Zeus & Rhea” line as the same identification.

Historical expressions such as “Name: Zeus & Rhea” and “Three keys. One family.” remain in the source. This companion does not modernize them. Addendum II’s sole-respondent and severance language governs the current attribution summary.
