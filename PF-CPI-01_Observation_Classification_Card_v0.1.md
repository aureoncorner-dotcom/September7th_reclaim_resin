## 4. Existing classification rule
**Gate 1 requires renewed, sustained supply-shock pass-through across more than one inflation print.** The September 6 ledger explicitly treats September 11 as the first possible new observation after the cooler frozen prints. One hot report cannot clear it.

No numerical CPI point forecast or hot/cool cutoff was located in the reviewed controlling baseline and live ledger. Do not invent one or turn the comparison anchors into thresholds. Describe the actual monthly change, composition and source-supported interpretation. Where a hot/cool assignment remains ambiguous, preserve a mixed or unresolved classification.

| Observed release pattern | Release classification | Gate / state effect |
|---|---|---|
| Hot print with source-supported renewed supply-shock pass-through | Supportive pass-through observation; persistence unconfirmed | Gate 1 NOT CLEARED. At most one new qualifying print at this node; no loop or phase promotion from this report alone. |
| Cool or contained print | RELIEF / COUNTER-SIGNAL | Gate 1 NOT CLEARED; A remains below ACUTE. Preserve relief explicitly. |
| Hot headline with mixed components, or supply-shock attribution unsupported | PARTIAL / AMBIGUOUS MATCH; retain any component relief | Gate 1 NOT CLEARED. Higher inflation alone does not establish the named mechanism. |
| Mixed relief and pressure without a clear overall direction | MIXED RELIEF / STATE UNCHANGED | Gate 1 NOT CLEARED; component findings remain visible. |
| Release unavailable or evidence insufficient to classify | UNRESOLVED / AWAITING EVIDENCE | No earned state change; record missing evidence. |

These labels implement the existing qualitative rule; they add no numerical gate. Headline, core and energy from one monthly release are components of one print, not multiple persistence observations. A revision is not another independent print. Earlier hot months cannot erase the intervening cool prints or manufacture renewed persistence.
