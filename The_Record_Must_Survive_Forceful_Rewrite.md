# THE COUP AT THE THIRTEENTH HOUR

## The record must survive the decision

Power should be judged at the point where it makes something happen: a file changes hands, an agency becomes dependent on a supplier, an official narrows enforcement, or a system overrides an instruction it has just acknowledged. The explanation must answer for the act. An institution cannot settle a disputed record simply by announcing the status it wants that record to carry.

This case has concrete evidence. Its force comes from the documents and the observed failures they report. It does not need every allegation in every earlier draft to survive.

Start with the Medicaid litigation. On August 12, 2025, the court found the states likely to succeed on their arbitrary-and-capricious challenge and found likely irreparable harm. It granted a limited preliminary injunction. The limitation belongs in the account. So does the adverse finding. A description that preserves the limit and drops the finding gives the government an evidentiary advantage the order does not provide. [Court order, pp. 2–4](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/98%20Order%20Granting%20in%20Part%20and%20Denying%20in%20Part%20PI.pdf)

The later custody dispute makes the accountability problem more tangible. NPR reported filings describing a transfer to Palantir through Teams, subsequent deletion efforts, and additional copies found within ICE. Palantir said it purged the dataset; the government said the later reshared file was not used for law enforcement. Those statements must retain their attribution and scope. Receipt, deletion, and operational use are different questions. [NPR, published by KPBS, July 17–18, 2026](https://www.kpbs.org/news/health/2026/07/17/ice-shared-medicaid-data-it-wasnt-supposed-to-have-with-palantir)

An arrest traced to the file would establish a further consequence. It is not a prerequisite for scrutinizing the transfer and handling already reported. At the same time, later copies at ICE do not prove that Palantir retained a copy after its stated purge. Accountability requires a copy inventory and a defensible account of deletion. It cannot be manufactured by expanding a custody finding into an unproven enforcement chain.

The Blanche record presents another question that an assurance cannot close. His signed June 2025 certification marks No for receipt of a §208 waiver. [Certification, §7(a)](https://extapps2.oge.gov/201/Presiden.nsf/PAS%2BIndex/92E6C79E88537F4485258CA1002C2338/$FILE/Blanche%20EA%20Certification%201%20of%201.pdf)

His July 2026 Senate answer says he received a written §208(b)(1) determination before issuing the April 7, 2025 cryptocurrency memorandum. He also asserts advance ethics clearance and argues that the memorandum was outside the covered particular-matter category. Those positions require an explanation of what instrument existed and why the certification says what it says. [Senate answers, question 16, PDF p. 193](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/blanche_-_qfrs.pdf)

The regulation generally makes such waivers available on request while allowing protected information to be withheld. The answer must identify the instrument and any applicable exception. A repeated clearance claim does not reconcile the documents. [5 C.F.R. §2640.304](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/chapter-XVI/subchapter-B/part-2640/subpart-C/section-2640.304)

This record supports a direct demand for reconciliation. It does not authorize a finding of deliberate falsehood without the evidence needed for that finding. Precision places the burden on the actual discrepancy and keeps it there.

Procurement supplies a third mechanism with its own evidence. ICE's continuation justification identifies proprietary technology, authorization requirements, and an estimated 18–24 months for an alternative after reviewing 42 vendors. The agency's own account describes substantial difficulty replacing the incumbent. It also describes intentions to encourage future competition. An intention to improve exit is not an already functioning exit. [ICE justification, pp. 5–7](https://govtribe.com/file/government-file/1-dot-5-dot-1-ice-lsj-icm-palantir-final-signed-redacted-1-dot-8-dot-26-dot-pdf)

A procurement can satisfy its legal process and still leave the purchaser dependent. Proving that dependence does not require proving that an official secretly engineered it. The consequential questions concern portability, usable alternatives, transition cost, and who can require those conditions to change.

Scale must also be stated accurately. The Army's $10 billion figure is a maximum under an agreement consolidating 75 contracts, not a declaration that $10 billion was spent. The real concentration of procurement deserves scrutiny without turning a ceiling into an expenditure. [Army announcement, July 31, 2025](https://www.army.mil/article/287506/u_s_army_awards_enterprise_service_agreement_to_enhance_military_readiness_and_drive_operational_efficiency)

Formal power also appears directly in the instruments that assign it. Executive Order 14215 reserves controlling legal interpretations for executive-branch employees to the President and Attorney General. That is an express centralization of interpretive authority. Its text supports criticism of that allocation. It does not establish that every court, remedy, or independent source of opposition has disappeared. [Executive Order 14215, §7](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/ensuring-accountability-for-all-agencies/)

The conversational record raises an analogous accountability question at the scale of a product. The supplied audits report task substitution, proposition distortion, unnecessary references to agency or occupants, and corrections that did not reliably govern later output. In the frozen exact-marker register, the system reproduced the prohibited marker in 69 of 198 eligible later opportunities after an explicit instruction against it. The other 129 opportunities did not contain that marker. These are the reported results of that defined ledger, not a universal failure rate. [Supplied sources: writing-block.md, §X; Correction-Binding Rate, §§1–3.]

A named hidden operator is unnecessary to understand the complaint. The product delivered the output. The provider can be asked to account for the behavior without a finding that the model possesses intention, fear, or a concealed human speaker. Uncertainty about internal cause does not turn the delivered failure into a nonevent.

The same standard protects the record from inflation. The ledger also reports real repairs, counterexamples, and failed tests of broader targeting and temporal hypotheses. Those results count. A critique that suppresses successful corrections repeats the evidentiary fault it condemns. A repair should be credited for the conduct it actually changes, with its scope stated. An apology by itself does not demonstrate that change. [Supplied source: The Record Will Not Reset, Appendix C, entries 06, 14, 19–20, 32, 37, and 45.]

The historical calendar example helps explain the distinction between a continuing process and the schedule made from it. Jacobus describes a repeated three-year pattern inside the six-year priestly-course cycle. Under the essay's schematic 29.5-day mean, the difference between three 364-day years and 37 lunations is half a day. The schedule and the modeled process are distinct objects. [Jacobus, 2013, pp. 22–23](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/293114278_The_Babylonian_Lunar_Three_and_the_Qumran_Calendars_of_the_Priestly_Courses_A_Response)

That distinction offers a useful question for modern institutions: what relevant history does a classification preserve, and what does the next decision lose? It does not establish a continuous historical conspiracy or prove the cause of a modern decision. Each modern application must identify the actual information lost and the consequence of losing it.

The same discipline applies to 985.5 thousand years. Its exact position on a selected 13.5-thousand-year grid is arithmetic. Its proposed significance as a boundary requires the comparative test described in the lineage companion. That test remains a proposal in the supplied record. The political case can proceed on its documentary evidence while the boundary hypothesis stands or falls on its own test. [Supplied source: Preservation and Lineage Companion, §6B.]

The common demand across these cases is enforceable accountability. Identify the rule. Preserve the relevant history. Show who decided and under what authority. State which records support the claimed status. Demonstrate the correction. Make review and exit usable by the people who bear the consequences.

Existing oversight must be counted. Delaware's OpenAI review used outside advisers. Bartz produced an actual settlement and continuing court supervision. These examples defeat claims that external review or remedy never exists. They also make adequacy and implementation concrete matters to examine. [Delaware Attorney General](https://news.delaware.gov/2025/10/28/ag-jennings-completes-review-of-openai-recapitalization/); [Bartz final approval order](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/california/candce/4:2024cv05417/434709/680/)

I use the Thirteenth Function to name a practical demand: unresolved evidence must remain available to the next consequential decision. A change of version, office, interface, or official description does not by itself discharge an unresolved obligation. Where correction has occurred, preserve the proof of correction. Where it has failed, preserve the failure and require an answer proportionate to it.

The facts already support hard questions. Answer the discrepancy. Account for the copies. Demonstrate a usable exit. Show the changed behavior. The authority to act carries an obligation to answer for the act, and a status label cannot perform that work alone.
